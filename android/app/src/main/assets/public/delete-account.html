📋 What happens when you delete your account?

Under Google Play Developer Data Safety policies, submitting this request initiates permanent deletion of your account and all associated records:

  • Permanent Deletion: Your Firebase user record, email, display name, and authentication tokens will be permanently deleted.
  • Study Data Purge: All Mistake Vault bookmarks, saved questions, chat conversations, and streak logs are deleted.
  • Cached Media: Any temporary scan images or cached session files are completely purged.
  • Timeframe: Requests submitted through this web form are processed and completed within 24–72 hours (well within Google Play's 30-day mandate).

⚠️ Important Note on Subscriptions: Deleting your HelpYou AI account does not automatically cancel active PRO subscriptions billed through Google Play. Please manage or cancel active subscriptions directly in the Google Play Subscriptions Center.
