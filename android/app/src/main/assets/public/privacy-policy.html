HelpYou AI Privacy Policy

Google Play Store & COPPA / CCPA Compliant

Effective Date: January 1, 2025 | Last Updated: March 2025

🛡️ Summary: HelpYou AI is built to empower students with intelligent study tools while maintaining absolute respect for your privacy. We never sell your personal data, never share your homework photos with third parties, and enforce strict automated data minimization.

1. Introduction

Welcome to HelpYou AI ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application and related services collect, use, store, and safeguard your personal information when you use the HelpYou AI application.

By accessing or using HelpYou AI, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue using the application.

2. Information We Collect & How We Use It

To provide high-quality educational services, we collect only strictly necessary data:

Data Category Specific Data Points Purpose & Retention
Account Credentials Email address, Display name, Unique User ID Used for account authentication, study streak sync, and security. Retained until account deletion.
Camera & Image Data Photos of homework, math problems, or study notes taken or selected via file picker Processed strictly via OCR and AI to generate step-by-step solutions. Images are temporary and purged automatically from our memory within 1 hour. We do NOT use your private images to train public AI models.
Audio & Microphone Voice audio inputs when using the AI Voice Tutor feature Streamed in real-time solely to convert speech into text prompts for educational answers. Raw audio is not permanently recorded or stored.
Educational Content Questions asked, Quiz progress, Mistake Vault questions Stored securely in Cloud Firestore so you can review academic mistakes, track streaks, and study offline.
Purchase & Billing Data In-app purchase receipts, subscription status Handled securely via Google Play In-App Billing and RevenueCat. We never collect or store your credit card or payment credentials directly.
Notifications & Study Reminders Local notification preferences, study streak schedules Used strictly to send optional daily study streak reminders and exam review alerts. Users can disable notifications anytime in Android device settings.
Technical & App Diagnostics Device model, OS version, anonymous crash logs Used solely to identify crashes, fix bugs, and ensure the app runs smoothly across all Android devices.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry leaders to provide core app functionality under strict data-protection agreements:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Cloud Firestore): For secure user login and encrypted cloud synchronization of study data.
  • Google Generative AI (Gemini): Powers academic problem solving, quiz generation, and essay evaluation. Prompts are transmitted over secure HTTPS/TLS.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: For managing PRO subscriptions and transactions in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

4. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

HelpYou AI is designed for students aged 13 and above. In strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child safety standards:

  • We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13.
  • If we discover or are notified that a user under 13 has created an account, we will immediately and permanently delete their account and associated data from our servers.
  • Parents or guardians can contact us at support@helpyou.ai to request inspection or immediate deletion of any child's data.

5. Generative AI Safety & Content Guidelines

Our app incorporates Generative AI to aid in learning. In accordance with Google Play's Generative AI Policy:

  • AI Disclaimers: HelpYou AI provides clear disclosures that AI-generated responses can occasionally contain inaccuracies. Students should independently verify critical academic concepts.
  • In-App User Reporting: Every AI-generated response includes a prominent reporting/flagging button. Users can immediately report any response that is inaccurate, offensive, harmful, or inappropriate. Reported content is reviewed for safety and model improvement.
  • Safety Guardrails: System prompts prohibit the generation of hate speech, sexually explicit material, violent content, self-harm instructions, or illegal guidance.

6. Account & Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users retain complete ownership and control of their personal information:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account directly at any time by navigating to Profile → Delete Account inside the app.
  • Web-Based Deletion Portal: You do NOT need the app installed to request deletion. You can request complete permanent deletion of your account, email, study progress, and vault entries via our public web portal: HelpYou AI Account Deletion Page.
  • Deletion Scope: Upon request, all personal identifiers, Firestore study records, Mistake Vault questions, and authentication credentials are permanently purged within 30 days.

7. Security of Your Information

We use industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data. All data transfers between the mobile app, backend servers, and AI endpoints are strictly encrypted using HTTPS / Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3). Real-time session locks ensure that your account can only be active on one mobile device at any given time.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new app features, regulatory requirements, or Google Play policy updates. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy and displaying a notice in the app.
